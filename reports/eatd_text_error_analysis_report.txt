INTERPRETATION
------------------------------------------------------------
The validation results are evaluated on the official EATD validation split.
Accuracy must be interpreted together with precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC and PR-AUC because the validation set contains substantially more non-depressed than depressed participants.
Threshold analysis is descriptive and does not constitute clinical threshold selection.
The model is a research baseline and must not be interpreted as a diagnostic system.
The text model currently shows weak discrimination for the depressed class on the held-out validation set.
Changing the classification threshold cannot by itself establish that the underlying text representation is clinically or scientifically adequate.
