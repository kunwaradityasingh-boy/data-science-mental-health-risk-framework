EATD Voice Baseline Model
=========================

Dataset: EATD-Corpus
Modality: Voice / Acoustic Features
Acoustic features: 142
Target: standardized SDS > 52
Training participants: 83
Validation participants: 79

Important limitations:
1. EATD is a relatively small dataset.
2. The target classes are imbalanced.
3. The official validation split is used as the held-out evaluation set.
4. Validation data was not used for model training.
5. The model is a research baseline and is not a clinical diagnostic system.
